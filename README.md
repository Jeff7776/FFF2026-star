# The Family Foreclosure Foundation (FFF)

**Official website:** https://TheFamilyForeclosureFoundation.Org/

## About the Foundation

The Family Foreclosure Foundation works to help bridge the gap between foreclosure and the possibility of returning to homeownership. Its mission centers on family stabilization, practical resource navigation, community partnerships, and helping families understand options that may be available to them.

The foundation's areas of focus include emergency food and housing resources, financial education, job training and placement, second-chance housing options, and surplus-asset recovery navigation.

Availability of services and eligibility may vary. Where services are provided by outside organizations, appropriate referrals and partnerships should be clearly identified.

## Website Project

This repository is intended to contain the source code and assets for the foundation's custom website and its Cloudflare Worker deployment.

The goal is to provide a polished, accessible, mobile-friendly experience that helps visitors:

- Learn about the foundation and its mission.
- Explore programs and community resources.
- Find information about surplus-asset recovery.
- Understand how to request assistance.
- Contact the foundation and explore ways to support its work.

## Quality and Safety Standards

The website should prioritize accessibility, responsive design, performance, secure handling of form submissions, accurate information, and working navigation.

Forms must not claim that information was submitted successfully unless the submission actually succeeded. Credentials and API keys must never be committed to the repository.

The existing live Worker must remain available as a fallback during development. DNS changes, production changes, and domain cutover require explicit approval.
